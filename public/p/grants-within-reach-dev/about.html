About

Good organizations deserve good opportunities.

But sustainable funding takes more than finding a grant and writing a strong proposal. It takes strategy, organizational readiness, strong programs, thoughtful partnerships, and the systems to manage funding well once it arrives. Grants Within Reach was created to help organizations build those pieces together.

Meet Deja

Hi, I'm Deja Hasness, and I believe that good grant work starts long before the application.

Nearly a decade in grants and nonprofit consulting has taught me that successful grant seeking doesn't happen in a silo. It intersects with programs, finances, leadership, evaluation, partnerships, and organizational strategy. That's why my work goes beyond the next deadline or application. I help organizations think strategically about what they're pursuing, why they're pursuing it, and whether they're truly positioned to succeed.

I'm a Grant Professional Certified (GPC) and Certified Nonprofit Professional (CNP) with experience across healthcare, human services, education, arts and culture, economic mobility, and community development. I work with organizations ranging from approximately $1 million to $30 million in annual operating budgets, bringing a practical understanding of the opportunities and challenges that come with organizations at different stages of growth.

Houston is home, and Houston-area nonprofits are at the heart of my work. I specialize in working with organizations across the Greater Houston region, with South Florida's Palm Beach County as a second base, while also bringing experience with nonprofits and funders in other communities. Grants Within Reach works with clients beyond Houston, too, when the partnership is the right fit.

I'm also a speaker and trainer who loves making grants feel a little less complicated, and a lot more strategic. Through Grants Within Reach, I bring all of those pieces together to help nonprofits build stronger strategies, pursue better-aligned opportunities, and create the capacity to manage funding well.

Because the goal isn't to chase more grants. It's to put the right grants within reach.
