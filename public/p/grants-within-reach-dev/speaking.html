Meet your speaker

From nurse to grant strategist.

Hi, I'm Deja Hasness. Nearly a decade in grants and nonprofit consulting has taught me that successful grant seeking doesn't happen in a silo. It intersects with programs, finances, leadership, evaluation, partnerships, and organizational strategy.

I'm also a speaker and trainer who loves making grants feel a little less complicated, and a lot more strategic.

  • Grant Professional Certified (GPC)
  • Certified Nonprofit Professional (CNP)
  • 9 years in grants and nonprofit consulting
