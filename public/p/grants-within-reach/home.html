How I work

All strategy, less chase.

Good organizations deserve good opportunities. Sustainable funding takes more than a strong proposal: it takes strategy, readiness, strong programs, thoughtful partnerships, and the systems to manage funding well once it arrives.

All strategy, less chase

Not every grant is the right grant. We focus on fit, alignment, and where your organization has the strongest chance of success.

Partnership over transactions

The best consulting relationships feel like an extension of your team.

Capacity matters

Winning funding only works when an organization is prepared to manage it successfully.

Practical, not theoretical

Recommendations should work in the real world, with real nonprofit budgets, staffing, and constraints.

Meet Deja

Good grant work starts long before the application.

Hi, I'm Deja Hasness. Nearly a decade in grants and nonprofit consulting has taught me that successful grant seeking doesn't happen in a silo. It intersects with programs, finances, leadership, evaluation, partnerships, and organizational strategy.

I work with organizations from roughly $1 million to $30 million in annual budget, across healthcare, human services, education, arts and culture, economic mobility, and community development. Houston is home, and Houston-area nonprofits are at the heart of my work.
