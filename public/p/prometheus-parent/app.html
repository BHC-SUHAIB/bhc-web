First, you. Breathe with this.

Milo is melting down over homework again. Here's your 30-second plan — you've got this.

1

Steady yourself first (10 sec)

Drop your shoulders, unclench your jaw. Milo is co-regulating off your body. Calm is contagious.

2

Name it, don't fix it

Get low, soft voice. Validate before redirecting — it lowers the alarm in his brain.

“This homework feels like too much right now. That makes sense. I'm right here.”
3

Shrink the task

His working memory is flooded. Offer one tiny next step, not the whole worksheet.

“Let's just do the first problem together. That's it for now.”
