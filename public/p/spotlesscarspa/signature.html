§ 03 — The Process

Seven steps, by hand, every car.

It's the same process whether you're in a Civic or a Cayenne. Skip a step and you scratch the paint — so we don't.

01

Foam-cannon pre-soak

A thick blanket of pH-neutral foam softens road grime so we lift it instead of grinding it across the clearcoat.
02

Two-bucket hand wash

Soap bucket, rinse bucket, grit guard. Plush mitts only. Wheels and lower panels wash last with their own mitt.
03

Clay-bar decontamination

Iron dust, brake dust, tree sap, tar — clay pulls every embedded contaminant out of the clearcoat.
04

Hand-dry & inspect

Soft microfibre, panel by panel. Every cuff of trim and door shut checked under shop light.
05

Premium wax — black or cherry

Two carnauba-rich waxes, applied with foam pad and hand-buffed. Pick the gloss profile that matches your paint.
06

Interior vacuum & dress

Crevice tool through every joint, dashboard wiped with UV-safe dressing, factory-fresh scent on the way out.
07

Final walk-around

Glass streaks. Tire dressing. Window jambs. Your car doesn't leave the bay until we sign off on it.
