What it does

Your Teachable students, finally portable

Teachable shows your students but won't let you take them cleanly — its export emails you a CSV that expires in about 24 minutes. Sidecar fixes that, without leaving the dashboard.

CSV & Excel

Export your students list to a clean CSV (free) or a formatted Excel .xlsx in seconds.

PDF report & Insights

A branded student & revenue report, plus totals and top students — right above your table.

Full export

Every field Teachable stores per student — phone, tags, sign-in stats, address — across every page, not just the columns on screen.

Private by design

Everything runs in your browser. No analytics, no servers — your student data never leaves your device.
