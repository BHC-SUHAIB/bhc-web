What it does

Your Thinkific students, finally portable

Thinkific shows your students a page at a time. Sidecar gives you the whole list, in the format you want, without leaving the dashboard.

CSV & Excel

Export your Users list to a clean CSV (free) or a formatted Excel .xlsx in seconds.

PDF report & Insights

A branded student report, plus totals and your top students, right above your table.

Full export

Every student across every page of your Users list, all 11 columns, into one spreadsheet. Not just the page on screen.

Private by design

Everything runs in your browser. No analytics, no servers. Sidecar sends nothing and fetches nothing extra, it just reads the page you are on.
